# Pattern play — mock-up (21 Oct 2026)

Drag the six real leather pieces onto an 18 × 18 cm tile, turn them 45° at a time, colour them, and see the tile
repeat 2×2, 3×3 or 5×5 as a pattern. "ทำเป็นรูป" draws a 1080 px picture to long-press and share.
Piece outlines and slits are read from the die files (`../diecut-leather-20261014/svg`, 1:1 mm); names from `brand.py`.

    python3 play-20261021/build.py      # play.html -> site/index.html
